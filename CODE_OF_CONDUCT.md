# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Everyone who participates in this repository (issues,
pull requests, reviews, discussions) is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep
discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing other
people's private information, including financial data, are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at **admin@crx.money**. Reports
are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and
may ban contributors temporarily or permanently, following the enforcement
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
